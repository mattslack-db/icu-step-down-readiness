The problem

A blocked ICU bed is a clinical and financial bottleneck

70–90%
Typical ICU occupancy — one blocked bed delays a critical admission
Manual
Step-down decisions are subjective and vary by provider and shift
"To be safe"
Clinicians hold patients longer than needed, lengthening stays

The question every intensivist asks on rounds — “who can safely move to step-down today?” — has no consistent, data-driven answer.

The outcome we sell

Free ICU capacity without compromising safety

10–15%
Target reduction in ICU length of stay
$2–5k
Saved per accelerated discharge-day
+beds
More ICU capacity for emergent admissions
Consistency
Evidence-informed decisions across shifts

For a 30-bed ICU, a 0.8-day average LOS reduction is on the order of hundreds of additional patient-days of capacity per year.

What we built

One governed journey — raw data to a clinician's screen

MIMIC-III (Delta Share)→ Lakeflow medallion→ Unity Catalog→ Lakebase serving→ ML + Gen AI→ Genie→ Databricks App

Integrated, not siloed: the same governed gold tables feed the operational app, the model, and natural-language querying — one lineage, one source of truth.

The product

A ranked ICU census the care team acts on

  • Census dashboard — every current patient ranked by a relative readiness index, color-banded Ready / Borderline / Not-ready.
  • Patient detail — the factors driving each recommendation, vital-sign trends, and a plain-language AI clinical summary.
  • Analytics — capacity and outcome trends, with honest model performance.

For the technical / clinical owner

Built to be trusted, governed, and operated

Governed by design

Unity Catalog across the medallion — PII classification tags on identifiers, least-privilege grants, and full bronze→gold lineage.

Operationally served

Gold features synced to Lakebase (Postgres) for low-latency app reads; model served on Mosaic AI; narrative via the Foundation Model API.

Reproducible

Everything deploys via Databricks Asset Bundles; 117 backend + 32 frontend + 17 pipeline-integration tests.

Safe by framing

De-identified public data; a relative decision-support index, not an automated discharge gate.

Model honesty

Decision support, not a discharge switch

AUC 0.66
Honest, modest discrimination on the retrospective feature set
Index, not %
Relative readiness rank within the current census — never a "probability of safe discharge"
Explainable
SHAP factors in clinical language; known risks always shown as risks

The roadmap to stronger discrimination: richer real-time features, calibration, and prospective validation with the clinical team.

The ask

From prototype to a unit pilot

  • Sponsor: a 60–90 day pilot in one ICU, measured on LOS and safe-transfer rate.
  • Clinical owner: co-define the readiness features and validate against your step-down criteria.
  • Platform: point the same pipeline at your EHR feed — the architecture is unchanged.
